01 / The person behind the code

Curiosity is
the starting point.

I’m Mohamed Isfak, a second-year Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Jaffna, Sri Lanka, working towards a career in MLOps and ML Systems Engineering.

I’m curious about what happens beyond a model’s prediction: how it becomes a useful application, how it gets deployed, and how it stays reliable. That curiosity guides my learning across backend development, edge AI, containers and cloud technologies.

Through individual and team projects, I’m building my foundations one system at a time. My goal is to create AI-powered software that is useful, secure and easy to maintain.

Outside my coursework, I serve as Community & Outreach Lead for the AWS Student Builder Group at the University of Jaffna.
